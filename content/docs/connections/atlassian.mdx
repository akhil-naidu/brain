title: Atlassian
description: Connect Atlassian via official MCP with dynamic client registration.
---

**Auth:** OAuth + dynamic client registration — no client id/secret env vars required.

**MCP:** `https://mcp.atlassian.com/v1/mcp/authv2`

## Connect

1. Open **`/tools`** → **Atlassian**  
2. Click **Connect** and complete the provider sign-in  
3. Enable the app for your chat (Agent mode)  
4. **Disconnect** when you want to revoke the grant in Brain  

## Callback URL

Register (or allow) Menu Connect callback:

```text
{BRAIN_PUBLIC_URL}/api/connections/atlassian/callback
```

Use your real origin (not `localhost`) in production. Mid-turn auth may print an exact redirect URI under `.eve/`.

Atlassian Rovo MCP for Jira / Confluence style workflows.

Docs: [Atlassian remote MCP](https://support.atlassian.com/atlassian-rovo-mcp-server/docs/getting-started-with-the-atlassian-remote-mcp-server/).
