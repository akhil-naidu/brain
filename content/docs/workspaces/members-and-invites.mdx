title: Members and invites
description: Invite teammates, change roles, and manage people in a team workspace.
---

## Invite someone

1. Open **`/workspaces/settings?tab=invites`**  
2. Create an invite (optional email)  
3. Share the link — or rely on SMTP if configured  

Invite roles are **admin** or **member** only (not owner).

Recipients open **`/invite/[token]`**, then sign in or register. Instance signup policy may be invite-only — invite registration still works for valid tokens.

Without SMTP, copy the invite link manually. With SMTP (`BRAIN_EMAIL_FROM` + `BRAIN_SMTP_*`), Brain can email the link.

## People

**`/workspaces/settings?tab=people`:**

- Change roles (with admin constraints)  
- Remove members  
- Transfer ownership (owner only)  

Admins cannot demote or remove other admins, and cannot change the owner’s role.
