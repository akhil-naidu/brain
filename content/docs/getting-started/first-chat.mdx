---
title: First chat
description: Sign in, pick a model, and send your first message in Brain.
---

After [local quickstart](/docs/getting-started/quickstart) or a deploy:

1. Open **`/setup`** (empty database) or **`/sign-in`**  
2. Go to **`/chat`**  
3. Confirm a model is selected (or add one under **`/models`**)  
4. Leave mode on **Agent** (or switch to **Ask** for plain chat)  
5. Send a short message
