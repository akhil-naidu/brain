---
title: SCIM
description: Provision users with a SCIM token after SSO is configured.
---

SCIM requires a licensed SSO entitlement **and** a configured SSO provider on the workspace.

1. **`/workspaces/settings?tab=sso`** → SCIM section  
2. Generate a SCIM token (revoke anytime)  
3. Point your IdP at the displayed SCIM base URL  

Use with [SSO](/docs/enterprise/sso) for company directory sync.
