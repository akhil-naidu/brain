---
title: SSO
description: Configure OIDC or SAML company sign-in for a team workspace.
---

Requires SSO entitlement on a licensed instance.

1. Open **`/workspaces/settings?tab=sso`** as workspace owner/admin  
2. Configure **OIDC** or **SAML**  
3. Add and verify email domains (DNS verification)  
4. Users choose **company SSO** on **`/sign-in`**  

Instance signup mode may be set to `sso-only` under [Instance policies](/docs/enterprise/instance-policies).
