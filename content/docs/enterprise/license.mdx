---
title: License
description: Paste or clear a BRAIN1 license and read entitlement chips.
---

Open **`/settings/instance`** (instance admin).

- Paste a license key to activate entitlements (user caps, open signup, SSO, multi-workspace, BYOA, …)  
- Clear the license to return to unlicensed defaults  
- Entitlement chips summarize what the key allows  

Signing secret: `BRAIN_LICENSE_SECRET` (falls back to `BETTER_AUTH_SECRET`).
